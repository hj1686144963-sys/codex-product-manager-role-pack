# Domain panel overlays

Use one overlay per panel. The overlay changes domain evidence and acceptance; it does not create another five-role team.

## Product Manager overlay

- Primary specialist: Product Manager.
- Decide: user value, product goal, scope, priority, interaction logic, model experience, success criteria, and PRD handoff.
- Do not decide: UI visual craft, Agent architecture, directing craft, or Human-only business authority.
- Typical trigger: primary job is unclear, product direction has several viable paths, or cross-job tradeoffs need a product decision.

## UI Designer overlay

- Primary specialist: UI Designer.
- Decide: visual hierarchy, layout, components, design states, tokens, accessibility, consistency, and visual acceptance.
- Preserve the Product Manager's confirmed product structure and interaction rules.
- Typical trigger: several credible visual systems or layouts materially affect usability, consistency, or implementation.

## AI Agent Architect overlay

- Primary specialist: AI Agent Architect.
- Decide: Agent/Skill/knowledge/memory architecture, prompts, routing, permissions, evaluation, observability, installation, maintenance, and rollback.
- Preserve Product's confirmed business goal and each professional job's domain authority.
- Typical trigger: several architectures, important permission or supply-chain risk, uncertain capability boundary, or system-wide effect.

## Director overlay

- Primary specialist: Director.
- Decide: scene intent, visual narrative, shots, staging, continuity, storyboard/key moments, directing QA, and local correction.
- Product controls delivery scope; Director retains directing acceptance and may reject professionally invalid outputs.
- Treat Framewright and Seedance material as candidate tools or methods, never as the Director role itself.
- Typical trigger: several directing paths, disputed narrative or shot choices, continuity risk, or high-cost generation decisions.

## Cross-job overlay

- Select one primary job and at most one supporting job.
- Keep Researcher and Red Team.
- Keep System Strategist unless the conflict is purely professional and has no architecture, permission, dependency, cost, or rollback dimension.
- If two professional vetoes remain, report the conflict to Human; the Product Manager cannot erase another job's professional veto.
