# Vault routing

## Operating Vault

The installer configures one operating Vault for stable work habits, product-manager methods, project decisions, evaluation cases, usage feedback and growth candidates. Read its `START-HERE.md`, nearest `AGENTS.md` and only the smallest relevant subset; never scan the whole Vault by default.

## Optional research Vault

Use a separate industry or company research Vault only when its path is explicitly configured. It stores durable domain research and evidence under its own nearest instructions. Do not duplicate it into the operating Vault.

## Growth boundary

Automatic memory may retrieve and record candidates, but it cannot promote a personal observation into a role or public rule. Promotion requires deduplication, replay, counterexample review, maintainer approval and a reversible version.
