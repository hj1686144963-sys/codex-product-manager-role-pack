# Capability promotion policy

## Layers

- Personal candidate: a single user's preferences, examples, mistakes, and working habits.
- Role candidate: recurring patterns observed across multiple people in the same role.
- Public capability: a versioned method or Skill that has evidence, tests, documentation, privacy review, rollback, and maintainer approval.

## Weekly review

Automation may collect and summarize candidates once per week. It must not edit public Skills or publish a release automatically.

The maintainer decides whether to reject, keep observing, merge into a role candidate, or approve for a public release. Approved changes should include evidence scope, version change, migration impact, and rollback notes.
